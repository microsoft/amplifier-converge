# Background reading

Brian Krabach’s [Your AI Built the First Version. Now What?](https://medium.com/@paradox921/your-ai-built-the-first-version-now-what-c477a3259607) and [Everyone on Your Team Can Build More. How Do You Build Together?](https://medium.com/@paradox921/everyone-on-your-team-can-build-more-how-do-you-build-together-39a9ec17fb5e) supply the individual and team collaboration framing.

Sam Schillace’s [You’re the Executive Now](https://sundaylettersfromsam.substack.com/p/youre-the-executive-now) and [It’s always hard that’s good](https://sundaylettersfromsam.substack.com/p/its-always-hard-thats-good) develop the executive judgment and peer coordination perspective.

Brian Krabach’s [Building Software with AI: A LEGO-Inspired Vision](https://medium.com/@paradox921/building-software-with-ai-a-lego-inspired-vision-2c40772309a4) supplies the focused-parts, explicit-connections, and parallel-assembly analogy.
