# Verification and delivery

For agents doing and checking work, the owner of the combined result, and people relying on delivery. The promise: “done” means the agreed outcome is supported by evidence.

**Example:** A request saves correctly, but the status screen still shows the previous owner. A passing storage test cannot establish that the handoff experience works.

## Commitments

1. **Check against the promise.** Identify meaningful success and failure cases before claiming completion. Cover the intended experience, agreed design choices, and relevant quality constraints. Check shared client promises on each supported platform, including specified settings, defaults, and persistence. Following a technology choice and demonstrating its expected benefit are separate checks. If a check cannot detect a plausible violation, improve it or state its limit.

2. **Exercise the delivered result.** Agents use representative user journeys, including relevant failure cases, and inspect the outcomes. Check the version people will receive. Repair failures and recheck; people are not the routine test workforce.

3. **Verify beyond the producer’s report.** The coordinating workflow independently examines or reruns meaningful checks, then verifies the combined result after integration. Several passing contributions do not prove their connections work. Use complementary review where it can expose consequential blind spots.

4. **Keep evidence honest and inspectable.** Record which promise was checked, against what version and conditions, who checked it and when, what was observed, and what remains uncertain. Distinguish met, not yet delivered, contradicted, deliberately unresolved, and unverified promises. Only explicitly open questions are deliberately unresolved. Missing or stale evidence is not a pass. Make the supporting checks and results reachable from a summary, without requiring everyone to inspect every detail. Investigate missing or conflicting evidence: compare versions and conditions, reproduce the behavior, and improve or repeat the check. If it remains unresolved within the work’s limits, keep the affected claim unverified and use [Ownership and collaboration](ownership-and-collaboration-contract.md)’s decision boundaries; uncertainty alone is not a request for human approval.

5. **Separate completion from release authority.** A worker report, verified outcome, and authorized release are distinct. Delivery stays within delegated authority and identifies remaining limitations, any needed decision, and who owns ongoing support or recovery. People retain consequential judgment, including whether the evidence deserves confidence.

**Check:** Introduce a failure between two otherwise working parts. The combined check should catch it. Remove a required check or use evidence from an older version: the completion claim should disclose the gap. Passing checks alone must not authorize an undelegated release.

**Left open:** Testing tools, evidence storage, and reviewer arrangement. Required observations follow the promised outcome, not a preferred tool.
